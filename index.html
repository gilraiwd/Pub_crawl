Venues come from OpenStreetMap (amenity = bar or pub): live for the radius mode, or from the prebuilt data/ files for state and nationwide. If more are found than Max venues, the ones nearest your start are kept. Hours come from each venue's OSM opening_hours when they can be parsed; otherwise the default window and last call are assumed, and the Source column says which. The plan visits all of them: it waits overnight when venues are closed, and rests when the next drink would pass your BAC limit (0 turns the limit off). Rides over 2 hours add a 20-minute driver break per 2 hours.
